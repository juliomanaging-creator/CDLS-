Some plants naturally absorb EXTREME concentrations of metals:

Examples:
- Alyssum bertolonii: Absorbs NICKEL (up to 1% dry weight!)
- Thlaspi caerulescens: Absorbs ZINC & CADMIUM
- Pteris vittata: Absorbs ARSENIC (used for soil remediation!)
- Berkheya coddii: Absorbs NICKEL (used for phytomining!)

Mechanism:
1. Roots absorb metal ions from soil
2. Transport to shoots via xylem (water transport vessels)
3. Sequester in vacuoles (storage compartments in cells)
4. Prevent toxicity via chelation (binding with proteins)

Proof of concept: If plants can absorb toxic metals at 1% concentration,
they can definitely absorb beneficial conductive materials! ✅
