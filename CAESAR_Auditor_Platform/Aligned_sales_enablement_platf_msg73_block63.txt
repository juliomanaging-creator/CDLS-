Once you hit 500-1,000 dealers, growth accelerates naturally:

Mechanisms:

A. Platform Network Effects:
├─ More dealers → More data → Better platform
├─ Better platform → More valuable for everyone
├─ More valuable → Easier to recruit new dealers
└─ Flywheel effect (exponential growth)

B. Marketplace Liquidity:
├─ More dealers → More carbon credits listed
├─ More liquidity → Better prices
├─ Better prices → More buyers (oil companies)
├─ More buyers → Higher credit values
└─ Higher values → More dealers join

C. Social Proof:
├─ 1,000 dealers → "Everyone's doing it"
├─ FOMO kicks in (fear of missing out)
├─ Laggards feel pressure to join
└─ Exponential adoption curve

Result: Organic growth 2-3x paid growth
Year 2: 2,000 dealers (2x Year 1)
Year 3: 5,000 dealers (2.5x Year 2)
