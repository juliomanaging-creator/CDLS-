Base Formula: 35% × Qualified Wages

Qualified Wages = Wages between 150% and 350% of CA minimum wage

For 2025: $16.50/hr minimum wage
- Lower threshold: $24.75/hr ($51,480 annually)
- Upper threshold: $57.75/hr ($120,120 annually)

Maximum annual credit per employee:
($57.75 - $24.75) × 2,000 hours × 35% = $23,100/year
Maximum 5-year credit per employee: $115,500
