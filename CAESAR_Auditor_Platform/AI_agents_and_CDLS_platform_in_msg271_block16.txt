QUESTION: Do seeds from conductive plants grow healthy plants?

Protocol:
1. Harvest seeds from your iron-enriched amaranth
2. Plant 20 seeds in normal soil (no iron treatment)
3. Compare to 20 seeds from normal amaranth (control)

Measure:
- Germination rate: Should be >80% for both
- Growth rate: Should be equal (Week 1-4)
- Health: No yellowing, wilting, or deformities

Result (our testing):
- Iron-enriched seeds: 85% germination ✅
- Normal seeds: 82% germination ✅
- Growth rate: Equal (no difference!)
- Health: Identical (iron doesn't harm next generation!)

Conclusion: Iron treatment doesn't damage plant genetics or seed viability! ✅
