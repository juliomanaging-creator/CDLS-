CELLULAR DAMAGE:
- Free copper ions (Cu²⁺) generate reactive oxygen species (ROS)
- ROS damage cell membranes, DNA, proteins
- Threshold: >20 ppm copper in animal tissues (toxic)
- Symptoms: Liver damage, kidney failure, neurological issues

BIOACCUMULATION:
- Animals eat copper-rich plants
- Copper accumulates in liver, kidneys
- Predators eat contaminated prey
- Magnification up food chain (biomagnification)

Example:
- Plant: 1,000 ppm copper (1% dry weight)
- Herbivore eats plant: 100 ppm copper in tissues
- Predator eats herbivore: 500 ppm copper (concentrated!)
- Top predator: 2,000+ ppm (TOXIC!) ❌
