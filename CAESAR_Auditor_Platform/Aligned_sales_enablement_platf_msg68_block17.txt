Question: Can carbon credits be tokenized under CARB rules?

Current CARB Rules:
- Credits tracked in LRT-CBTS system (CARB's database)
- Transfers require CARB approval
- Quarterly reporting mandatory

Our Approach: Dual-Ledger System
- CARB system = Legal record (source of truth)
- Blockchain = Trading record (marketplace)
- Smart contract syncs with CARB
- Quarterly settlements (blockchain → CARB)

Process:
1. Generate credits (CARB verifies)
2. Mint NFT (blockchain proof)
3. Trade on blockchain (24/7)
4. Settle to CARB (quarterly batch)

Legal Structure:
- CDLS is registered LCFS entity
- Maintains CARB compliance
- Blockchain is "internal ledger"
- CARB sees final settled positions

Precedent:
- Toucan Protocol (tokenized carbon offsets)
- KlimaDAO (on-chain carbon marketplace)
- Both operating legally in compliance markets
