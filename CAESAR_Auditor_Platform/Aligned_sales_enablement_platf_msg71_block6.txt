Borrowing Against $CDLS:
- Deposit $CDLS on Aave, Compound, or MakerDAO
- Borrow USDC at 60% LTV (loan-to-value)
- Use USDC to buy EV haulers
- Keep $CDLS (no dilution)

Example:
Dealer holds $100,000 in $CDLS
Deposits on Aave
Borrows $60,000 USDC (60% LTV)
Uses $60,000 as down payment on $230,000 hauler
Hauler generates $5,000/month revenue
After 12 months: Repays loan
Still owns $CDLS (now worth $200,000)
ROI: 300%

vs. Traditional:
Sell $60,000 in equity to buy hauler
Diluted permanently
Lower ROI
