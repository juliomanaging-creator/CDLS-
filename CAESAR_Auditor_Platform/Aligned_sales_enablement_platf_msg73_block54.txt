Streamlined Onboarding:

Process:
1. KYC Verification (5 minutes)
   ├─ Upload ID (Persona)
   ├─ Selfie verification
   └─ Auto-approved (95% of cases)

2. Wallet Setup (10 minutes)
   ├─ Create MetaMask wallet
   ├─ Or use WalletConnect (mobile)
   └─ Backup seed phrase (security)

3. Purchase Tokens (15 minutes)
   ├─ Buy ETH on Coinbase
   ├─ Transfer to MetaMask
   ├─ Swap ETH for $CDLS on Uniswap
   └─ Or: Direct purchase with credit card (Moonpay)

4. Stake for Platform Access (5 minutes)
   ├─ Visit cdls.network/stake
   ├─ Connect wallet
   ├─ Stake 10,000 $CDLS (Starter tier)
   └─ Confirm transaction

Total Time: 35 minutes (vs. 6-12 months traditional)
