Auto dealers face 3 converging crises:

1. REGULATORY MANDATES
   - California ACF: 100% ZEV sales by 2035
   - Logistics must convert to match
   - $47B transition cost nationwide

2. ECONOMIC UNCERTAINTY  
   - Diesel costs volatile ($3.50-$6.00/gallon)
   - EV ROI calculations complex
   - No trusted decision tools

3. CARBON CREDIT CONFUSION
   - $500K-$750K/year available per dealer
   - 90% of dealers unaware
   - No infrastructure to capture value

Total addressable pain: $50B/year
