Problem: I-5 corridor needs sensors (traffic, temperature, power monitoring)
Solution: Bio-battery powered sensors using conductive root systems!

Deployment:
- Plant bio-battery plants along corridor (every 50 meters)
- Each plant generates 1-5 mW continuous power (photosynthesis-driven)
- Connect to ultra-low-power sensors (environmental monitoring)
- Wireless data transmission (LoRaWAN, low power wide area network)

Sensors powered:
- Temperature: 0.1 mW (monitors corridor surface temperature)
- Traffic counter: 1 mW (vehicle detection, usage statistics)
- Power monitor: 0.5 mW (wireless charging efficiency, real-time data)
- Weather station: 2 mW (wind, rain, humidity)

Benefits:
✅ Zero maintenance power: Self-charging via sunlight!
✅ Grid-independent: No electrical infrastructure needed
✅ Scalable: Plant 5,000+ along 270-mile corridor
✅ Data-rich: Real-time monitoring for operations & research

Cost: $50/sensor+plant (vs $500 for solar panel + battery backup)
ROI: 10× savings on sensor power infrastructure! ✅

Implementation timeline:
- Year 1: Pilot (50 sensors, Sacramento-Stockton section)
- Year 2-3: Scale (5,000 sensors, full I-5 corridor)
- Year 4+: Data-driven optimization (adjust based on sensor feedback)
