dealrooms
- id (UUID, PK)
- investor_id (FK to investors)
- deal_name (VARCHAR 200)
- deal_stage (ENUM: prospect, due_diligence, negotiation, closing, closed)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- updated_at (TIMESTAMP)
- settings (JSONB - branding, permissions, etc.)

documents
- id (UUID, PK)
- dealroom_id (FK)
- file_name (VARCHAR 255)
- file_type (VARCHAR 50)
- s3_key (VARCHAR 500)
- version (INTEGER)
- uploaded_by (FK to users)
- uploaded_at (TIMESTAMP)
- folder_path (VARCHAR 500)
- permissions (JSONB)

document_versions
- id (UUID, PK)
- document_id (FK)
- version_number (INTEGER)
- s3_key (VARCHAR 500)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- created_by (FK to users)
