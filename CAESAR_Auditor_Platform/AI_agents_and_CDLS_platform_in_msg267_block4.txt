Introduce carbon nanomaterials into plant:

CARBON NANOMATERIALS (Conductive + Biocompatible):
1. Single-Walled Carbon Nanotubes (SWCNT):
   - Conductivity: 10⁶-10⁷ S/m (excellent!)
   - Size: 1-2 nm diameter (small enough for plant uptake!)
   - Biocompatibility: High (carbon-based, non-toxic)
   
2. Graphene Quantum Dots (GQD):
   - Conductivity: 10⁵ S/m
   - Size: 2-10 nm diameter
   - Biocompatibility: Very high (used in medical applications)
   
3. Reduced Graphene Oxide (rGO):
   - Conductivity: 10⁴-10⁵ S/m
   - Size: 2D sheets, 1-10 nm thick
   - Biocompatibility: High

PROTOCOL:
1. Prepare nanoparticle solution:
   - Concentration: 100 mg/L (0.01%)
   - Surfactant: Add 0.1% Tween-20 (helps plant uptake)
   - Sonication: 30 minutes (disperses nanoparticles evenly)

2. Apply to plant:
   METHOD A (Root Absorption):
   - Hydroponic setup (plant roots in water, no soil)
   - Add nanoparticle solution to water
   - Exposure: 7-14 days (continuous uptake)
   
   METHOD B (Foliar Spray):
   - Spray nanoparticle solution on leaves
   - Stomata (leaf pores) absorb nanoparticles
   - Faster uptake, but less penetration to roots

3. Monitor uptake:
   - Day 3: Nanoparticles visible in leaves (dark color)
   - Day 7: Nanoparticles in stems
   - Day 14: Nanoparticles in roots (transported via phloem)

Expected result:
- Plant tissues: 0.01-0.1% carbon nanomaterial by weight
- Conductivity: 10⁴-10⁶ S/m (semi-conductor to conductor!)
- Photosynthesis: Enhanced 20-30% (MIT study confirmed!)
- Energy storage: Moderate (carbon nanotubes can store some charge)
