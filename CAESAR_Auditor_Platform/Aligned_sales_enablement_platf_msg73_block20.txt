IRS Classification: Property (utility token)

Taxable Events:
1. Earning $HAUL:
   ├─ Taxable as income when earned
   ├─ Fair market value on date earned
   └─ Reported as self-employment income (if dealer) or hobby income

2. Spending $HAUL:
   ├─ Disposition of property
   ├─ Capital gain/loss
   └─ Basis: FMV when earned

Example:
Earn 10,000 $HAUL for completing hauls
FMV at earning: $0.10 per token = $1,000 income
Pay tax: $300 (30% bracket)
Later spend 10,000 $HAUL on subscription
FMV at spending: $0.15 per token = $1,500
Capital gain: $500 ($1,500 - $1,000 basis)
Additional tax: $75 (15% capital gains)
Total tax: $375 on $10,000 $HAUL
