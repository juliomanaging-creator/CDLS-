Section 3. COMMISSIONER APPOINTMENT
The [Governor/Insurance Commissioner/Health Director] 
shall appoint the Commissioner to represent [State] on 
the Interstate Commission, subject to [confirmation by 
Senate/other state process].

Section 4. FUNDING AUTHORIZATION
The [appropriate state agency] is authorized to:
(a) Pay membership dues and assessments
(b) Participate in shared purchasing programs
(c) Contract with the Commission for services
(d) Accept and expend funds from the Commission

Section 5. RULE-MAKING AUTHORITY
The [Insurance Commissioner/Health Director] may adopt 
rules necessary to implement this compact, including:
(a) Data sharing procedures
(b) Provider participation requirements
(c) Administrative coordination

Section 6. EFFECTIVE DATE
This Act takes effect [immediately upon passage / July 1, 
20XX] and the compact becomes binding when [3] states 
have enacted substantially similar legislation.

Section 7. SEVERABILITY
If any provision of this Act is held invalid, the 
remainder shall not be affected.
