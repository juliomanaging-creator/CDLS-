Required data per quarter:
- VIN
- Miles driven (odometer readings)
- Electricity consumed (kWh from meter)
- Base carbon intensity of electricity source
- Any renewable energy credits (if using solar)

Example report for one vehicle:
VIN: 5YJSA1E14HF000001
Q1 2025 Miles: 12,500
Q1 2025 Electricity: 26,250 kWh
Utility: Pacific Gas & Electric
CI Score: 95.97 gCO2e/MJ (PG&E grid average)
Renewable %: 0% (or higher if using solar)
