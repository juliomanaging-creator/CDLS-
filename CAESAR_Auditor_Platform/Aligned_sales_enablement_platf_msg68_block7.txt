Solution:
├─ Transparent pricing (public order book, real-time)
├─ Instant settlement (smart contracts, seconds)
├─ Low fees (2% take rate, automated)
├─ Global buyers (anyone with crypto wallet)
├─ Fractional trading (trade 0.01 credits)
└─ Real-time generation (mint credits as miles driven)

How It Works:

Step 1: Credit Generation
- CDLS vehicle drives 100 miles
- Telematics automatically logs on-chain
- Smart contract calculates CO2 avoided
- Pre-mints "unverified" carbon credit NFT

Step 2: Quarterly Verification
- Submit data to CARB (traditional process)
- Receive official verification
- Smart contract upgrades NFT to "verified"
- Now tradable on marketplace

Step 3: Trading
- Dealer lists 100 verified credits at $105 each
- Buyer (oil company, DAO, individual) bids $104
- Smart contract matches orders
- Settlement instant (credit transferred, payment sent)
- 2% fee to CDLS treasury (in $CDLS tokens, burned)

Step 4: Delivery to CARB
- At quarter end, buyer withdraws credits from chain
- CDLS submits transfer to CARB on buyer's behalf
- Credits officially transferred in CARB system
- Blockchain record = proof for audit
