stakeholders
- id (UUID, PK)
- dealroom_id (FK)
- name (VARCHAR 200)
- title (VARCHAR 200)
- email (VARCHAR 255)
- phone (VARCHAR 50)
- organization (VARCHAR 200)
- role_type (ENUM: decision_maker, influencer, champion, blocker, neutral)
- influence_level (INTEGER 1-10)
- engagement_level (INTEGER 1-10)
- notes (TEXT)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
- updated_at (TIMESTAMP)

stakeholder_relationships
- id (UUID, PK)
- stakeholder_from_id (FK to stakeholders)
- stakeholder_to_id (FK to stakeholders)
- relationship_type (ENUM: reports_to, collaborates_with, influences)
- strength (INTEGER 1-5)

stakeholder_interactions
- id (UUID, PK)
- stakeholder_id (FK)
- interaction_type (ENUM: email, call, meeting, document_view)
- subject (VARCHAR 500)
- notes (TEXT)
- sentiment (ENUM: positive, neutral, negative)
- occurred_at (TIMESTAMP)
- created_by (FK to users)

stakeholder_tasks
- id (UUID, PK)
- stakeholder_id (FK)
- dealroom_id (FK)
- task_description (TEXT)
- assigned_to (FK to users - your team member)
- due_date (DATE)
- status (ENUM: pending, in_progress, completed, blocked)
- created_at (TIMESTAMP)
