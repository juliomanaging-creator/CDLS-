LAYER 1: CANOPY (Trees - Lightning Protection + Food)
Species: Valley Oak, Coast Live Oak (copper-doped for conductivity)
Function: Grounding, lightning protection
Wildlife value: Acorns feed 100+ species (deer, squirrels, jays!)
Safety: Copper in WOOD (not acorns), acorns safe to eat ✅

LAYER 2: UNDERSTORY (Shrubs - Conductivity + Habitat)
Species: Toyon, Coffeeberry (iron-enriched)
Function: Visual screening, moderate conductivity
Wildlife value: Berries feed birds (40+ species!)
Safety: Iron in leaves (birds tolerate, berries safe!) ✅

LAYER 3: GROUND COVER (Conductive Sensors + Pollinator Food)
Species: Amaranth, Kale (iron-enriched, conductive leaves)
Function: Low-power sensor connections, data collection
Wildlife value: Seeds/leaves feed birds, rabbits, deer
Safety: Iron is nutrient (animals benefit!) ✅

LAYER 4: SOIL MICROBES (Carbon Storage + Conductivity)
Amendment: Biochar, activated charcoal (10% soil volume)
Function: Improves soil conductivity for grounding
Wildlife value: Improves soil health, supports worms/insects
Safety: Charcoal is edible (detoxifying for animals!) ✅

RESULT: Multi-functional ecosystem!
- Technology: Conductive network, sensor power, grounding
- Ecology: Food web (acorns→deer, berries→birds, leaves→rabbits)
- Soil: Carbon-enriched, microbially active, supports biodiversity
- Climate: Carbon sequestration (trees + biochar = long-term storage)

Every component serves BOTH technology AND ecology! 🌳⚡🦌✅
