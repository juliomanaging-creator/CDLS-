WEEK 1: GERMINATION
1. Plant sunflower seeds in soil or hydroponic medium
2. Water with distilled water (no copper yet!)
3. Temperature: 20-25°C (room temperature)
4. Light: 12 hours/day (grow light or windowsill)

WEEK 2-4: COPPER INTRODUCTION (Gradual Increase!)
Week 2:
- Prepare copper solution: 10 ppm (10 mg/L) copper sulfate
- Water plant with copper solution (500 mL per plant, 2× per week)
- Monitor: Leaves should remain green (healthy)

Week 3:
- Increase concentration: 50 ppm copper sulfate
- Continue watering (500 mL, 2× per week)
- Monitor: Some yellowing acceptable (minor stress), but not wilting

Week 4:
- Increase concentration: 100 ppm copper sulfate
- Final push (maximize copper uptake)
- Monitor: If wilting occurs, reduce to 50 ppm

WEEK 5-6: MATURATION
- Maintain 100 ppm copper sulfate watering
- Plant should be 30-50 cm tall
- Root system well-developed (5-10 cm diameter)

WEEK 7: HARVEST & TEST
1. Carefully remove plant from soil (preserve roots intact!)
2. Rinse roots with distilled water (remove soil particles)
3. Pat dry with paper towel

CONDUCTIVITY TEST:
4. Set multimeter to resistance mode (Ω)
5. Place probes on root surface (2 cm apart)
6. Measure resistance:
   - Normal plant root: >1 MΩ (megaohm, very resistive)
   - Copper-doped root: 100 Ω - 10 kΩ (1,000-100,000× more conductive!)
   
7. Calculate conductivity:
   - Conductivity (S/m) = Length / (Resistance × Area)
   - Example: 2 cm apart, 1 kΩ, 1 cm² cross-section
   - Conductivity = 0.02 m / (1,000 Ω × 0.0001 m²) = 200 S/m
   
   SUCCESS: 100-1,000 S/m (semi-conductor range!) ✅

ANALYSIS:
8. Dry root tissue (oven at 60°C for 24 hours)
9. Weigh dry mass
10. Chemical analysis (optional, requires lab access):
    - Atomic absorption spectroscopy (AAS)
    - Measures copper concentration
    - Expected: 0.1-1% copper by dry weight

SAFETY:
- Copper sulfate is IRRITANT (wear gloves, goggles)
- Do not ingest plant (copper is toxic at high concentrations)
- Dispose of plant material properly (not in compost, copper contamination)
