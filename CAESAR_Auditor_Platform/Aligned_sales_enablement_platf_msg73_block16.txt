Tier 1: Basic KYC (All users)
├─ Email verification
├─ Phone number (SMS)
├─ Basic info (name, country)
└─ Limit: $1,000/month trading

Tier 2: Enhanced KYC (Higher limits)
├─ Government ID (passport, driver's license)
├─ Selfie verification
├─ Address proof
└─ Limit: $50,000/month trading

Tier 3: Institutional KYC (Unlimited)
├─ Business verification
├─ Beneficial ownership (25%+ owners)
├─ Financial statements
└─ Limit: Unlimited

Provider: Persona, Onfido, or Jumio
Cost: $1-5 per verification
Processing: Instant (automated) or 24 hours (manual review)
