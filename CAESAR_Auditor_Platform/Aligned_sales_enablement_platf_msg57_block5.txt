Credits = (Diesel CI - EV CI) × Energy × Credit Conversion Factor

Where:
- Diesel CI = 100.45 gCO2e/MJ (carbon intensity)
- EV CI = 95.97 gCO2e/MJ (varies by utility)
- Energy = kWh consumed × 3.6 MJ/kWh
- Credit Conversion Factor = 0.000001 metric tons/g

Simplified: ~0.89 credits per metric ton CO2 avoided
