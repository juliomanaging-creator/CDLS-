Delaware LP Structure:
├─ Pass-through entity (no entity-level tax)
├─ Income flows to partners
├─ K-1 forms issued annually
└─ Partners pay tax on their share

Token Revenue:
├─ Token sale proceeds: Not taxable income (capital raise)
├─ Platform subscriptions: Ordinary income
├─ Marketplace fees: Ordinary income
├─ Carbon credit sales: Ordinary income
└─ File Form 1065 (Partnership return)

Deductions:
✓ Developer salaries
✓ Marketing expenses
✓ Legal and compliance
✓ Infrastructure (AWS, etc.)
✓ Depreciation (vehicles, equipment)
