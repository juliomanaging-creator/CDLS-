BEST CANDIDATES (Edible + Hardy + Fast-Growing):

1. SUNFLOWER (Helianthus annuus):
   - Growth: 60-90 days
   - Edible parts: Seeds (birds, rodents love them!)
   - Iron uptake: Excellent (naturally accumulates iron)
   - Conductivity potential: 500-1,000 S/m with iron doping
   - Wildlife value: HIGH (seeds feed 40+ bird species!) 🐦✅

2. AMARANTH (Amaranthus spp.):
   - Growth: 60-75 days
   - Edible parts: Leaves (salad greens), seeds (grain)
   - Iron content: Naturally HIGH (10,000+ ppm, "iron-rich superfood!")
   - Conductivity potential: 1,000-2,000 S/m (already iron-rich!)
   - Wildlife value: Seeds feed birds, leaves feed deer ✅

3. QUINOA (Chenopodium quinoa):
   - Growth: 90-120 days
   - Edible parts: Seeds (complete protein, all amino acids!)
   - Iron uptake: Excellent (naturally high iron)
   - Conductivity potential: 800-1,500 S/m
   - Wildlife value: Seeds nutritious for birds 🦜✅

4. KALE (Brassica oleracea):
   - Growth: 55-75 days
   - Edible parts: Leaves (humans, deer, rabbits)
   - Iron content: 1,500 ppm (naturally high!)
   - Conductivity potential: 600-1,200 S/m
   - Wildlife value: Deer LOVE kale! 🦌✅
