Plants naturally absorb elements through roots via:

PASSIVE TRANSPORT (No Energy Required):
- Osmosis: Water + dissolved minerals move down concentration gradient
- Diffusion: Elements move from high → low concentration
- Examples: Water, oxygen, carbon dioxide

ACTIVE TRANSPORT (Energy Required, ATP-driven):
- Ion pumps: Plant expends energy to move minerals AGAINST gradient
- Selective uptake: Plant chooses which elements to absorb
- Examples: Potassium (K+), Nitrogen (NO3-), Phosphorus (PO4³⁻)

Key insight: Plants CAN absorb non-standard elements if:
✅ Element is water-soluble (dissolves in soil water)
✅ Element size fits through cell membrane channels (0.4-2 nanometers)
✅ Plant has transporters for that element (or similar element)
✅ Concentration gradient favors uptake
