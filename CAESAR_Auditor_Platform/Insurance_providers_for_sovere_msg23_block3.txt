TRANSFORMING AUTOMOTIVE LOGISTICS

California Dealer Logistics Solutions (CDLS) is building the world's first 
decentralized, AI-powered zero-emission automotive logistics network.

THE CHALLENGE
California's Advanced Clean Fleets mandate requires 100% zero-emission logistics 
by 2035, yet no existing infrastructure can efficiently manage electric vehicle 
fleets while addressing enterprise data sovereignty concerns.

OUR SOLUTION: JUDAS ARCHITECTURE
We've developed JUDAS (Joint Unified Dealer Autonomous System), a distributed 
AI platform where inference runs locally on Ollama nodes behind each partner's 
firewall. Raw dealership data remains on-premise, with only anonymized 
optimization metadata transmitted to our coordination layer.

This "edge-first" design delivers network-wide efficiency while maintaining 
complete data sovereignty—critical for the privacy-sensitive automotive industry.
