Monday:
□ Register with Delaware Division of Corporations
   - Online: https://corp.delaware.gov/
   - File: Certificate of Incorporation
   - Name: California Dealer Logistics Solutions, Inc.
   - Shares: 10,000,000 authorized ($0.0001 par value)
   - Directors: 1-3 (you can be sole director initially)
   - Registered Agent: Harvard Business Services ($50/yr)
   - Total Cost: $89 + $50 = $139

Tuesday:
□ Obtain new EIN from IRS
   - https://www.irs.gov/ein
   - Select "Corporation" as entity type
   - Purpose: "Operating company for logistics services"
   - Takes 5 minutes online

Wednesday:
□ File California Foreign Corporation Registration
   - Form S&DC S-100
   - Fee: $100
   - Due within 90 days but file immediately
   - Required to do business in California

Thursday:
□ Prepare Corporate Bylaws (template available free)
□ Issue founder stock (100% to yourself initially)
□ Adopt banking resolutions

Friday:
□ File for Sacramento business license
   - City of Sacramento Business Operations Tax (SBOT)
   - Online: https://www.cityofsacramento.org/Finance/Revenue/Business-Taxes
   - Fee: ~$50-$200 depending on revenue
   - Required before opening bank account

Result: C-Corp formed, California qualified, license obtained ✓
