Purpose: Tokenized LCFS carbon credits
Supply: Generated based on real vehicle miles
Standard: ERC-1155 (semi-fungible tokens)

How It Works:
1. CDLS fleet drives 1M miles in Q1
2. Generates 1,246 LCFS credits (verified by CARB)
3. Each credit minted as NFT (on-chain proof)
4. Metadata: Quarter, dealer, miles, verification
5. Credits tradable on blockchain marketplace

Advantages:
✓ Instant settlement (vs. 90 days for CARB)
✓ 24/7 trading (vs. business hours)
✓ Fractional ownership (trade 0.1 credits)
✓ Transparent pricing (public order book)
✓ Global buyers (not just CA oil companies)
✓ Smart contract escrow (trustless)

Marketplace:
- Built on CDLS platform
- 2% take rate (vs. 3% for brokers)
- Instant liquidity
- Price discovery (real-time market)
- Futures contracts (lock in future prices)
