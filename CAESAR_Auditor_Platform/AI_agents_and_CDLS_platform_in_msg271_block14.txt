Service: Waters Agricultural Laboratories (California)
Website: watersag.com
Test: "Complete Plant Tissue Analysis"
Cost: $25 per sample
Turnaround: 7-10 days

What they measure:
- Iron: ppm (should be 1,000-5,000 for conductive plants)
- Copper: ppm (should be <50 if using iron instead)
- Zinc: ppm
- Protein, vitamins, minerals (nutritional value)

How to submit:
1. Harvest 100g plant tissue (leaves, stems, roots separately)
2. Dry in oven (60°C, 24 hours)
3. Mail to lab ($25 fee + shipping)
4. Receive detailed report

Safety thresholds:
- Iron: <10,000 ppm (safe for wildlife)
- Copper: <100 ppm (safe for most animals)
- Zinc: <500 ppm (safe range)

If within thresholds: SAFE for wildlife consumption! ✅
