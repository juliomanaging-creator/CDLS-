1. EFTPS Enrollment:
   - Required for paying federal taxes electronically
   - Enroll at: https://www.eftps.gov/
   - Provide: C-Corp EIN, bank account info
   - Receive PIN by mail (7-10 days)

2. Uses:
   - Quarterly Form 941 payroll tax payments
   - Annual Form 1120 corporate tax payments
   - Estimated tax payments (Form 1120-W)

3. Required For:
   - Businesses with >$2,500 payroll tax liability
   - Claiming R&D payroll tax offset (Form 8974)
