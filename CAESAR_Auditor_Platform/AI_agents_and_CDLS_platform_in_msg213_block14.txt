Every heat engine is limited by:
η_max = 1 - (T_cold / T_hot)

For your system:
T_hot = 575 K (302°C compressed air)
T_cold = 288 K (15°C ambient)
η_max = 1 - (288/575) = 49.9%

Real efficiency: 49.9% × 65% = 32.4%
(The 65% accounts for real-world losses)

This is THERMODYNAMIC LAW - cannot be exceeded!
