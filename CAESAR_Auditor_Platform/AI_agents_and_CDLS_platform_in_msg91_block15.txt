1. Register for Sacramento Business Operations Tax (SBOT)
   - Website: https://www.cityofsacramento.org/Finance/Revenue/Business-Taxes
   - Online application available
   - Required documents:
     * Delaware Certificate of Incorporation
     * California Foreign Corporation Statement
     * Federal EIN
     * Business address (can use home address initially)
     * Description of business

2. Fee Calculation:
   - Based on gross receipts
   - Year 1 (no receipts): $50 minimum
   - Year 2+: Scales with revenue
   
3. Annual Renewal:
   - Due January 31 each year
   - Penalty if late: $50 + 10% per month

4. Business License Certificate:
   - Mailed within 2 weeks
   - Post at place of business
   - Provide copy to bank when opening account
