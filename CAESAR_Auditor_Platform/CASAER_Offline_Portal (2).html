Welcome to CASAER

CASAER provides enforcement pathway guidance and analytical tools for the California State Auditor's Office. This system guides auditors on what the right path is to look for information — it does not make enforcement decisions.

Offline Mode: This portal runs entirely in your browser. No data is transmitted externally. Upload data files for local analysis and download reports directly to your machine.

Quick Start

1. Select a Department from the Departments tab
2. Create a New Audit session with the hearing request scope
3. Upload financial data for Analytics (Benford's Law, anomaly detection)
4. Generate Enforcement Reports with findings and statute references
5. Download reports in PDF or CSV format
