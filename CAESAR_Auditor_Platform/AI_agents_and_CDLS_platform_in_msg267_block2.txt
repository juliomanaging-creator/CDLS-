Plants can absorb NANOPARTICLES through:

DIRECT UPTAKE:
- Particle size: <50 nanometers (passes through cell walls)
- Examples: Gold nanoparticles, silver nanoparticles, carbon nanotubes
- Pathway: Root → xylem → leaves

INDUCED UPTAKE (Enhanced by Surface Modification):
- Coat nanoparticles with surfactants (makes them hydrophilic)
- Plants mistake them for nutrients
- Absorption rate: 10-100× higher than uncoated particles

Precedent research:
- MIT (2017): Carbon nanotubes in spinach, enhanced photosynthesis 30%!
- UC Riverside (2019): Silver nanoparticles in wheat, antibacterial properties
- Stanford (2020): Graphene quantum dots in plants, enhanced conductivity

This is PROVEN - plants can absorb and integrate conductive nanomaterials! ✅
