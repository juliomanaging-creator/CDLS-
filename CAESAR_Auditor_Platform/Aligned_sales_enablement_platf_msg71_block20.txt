Problem: Dealer has 10 credits, buyer wants 1,000
Solution: Pool multiple dealers' credits

Process:
1. 100 dealers each deposit 10 credits
2. Pool mints "Pooled Credit Token" (fungible)
3. Buyer buys from pool (instant, no fragmentation)
4. Pool splits payment to 100 dealers

Benefits:
✓ Liquidity for small generators
✓ Simplicity for large buyers
✓ Lower transaction costs
