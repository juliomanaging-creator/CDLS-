1. □ File California LLC dissolution (Form LLC-4/7)
   - Online: https://bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov/
   - Takes 10 minutes
   - Cost: $0
   
2. □ Notify IRS of LLC closure
   - Letter template: "California LLC [Entity #B20250412694] 
     was dissolved on [date]. Please close EIN [your EIN]. 
     No assets, no liabilities, no activity."
   - Mail to IRS address for your state
   
3. □ Begin Delaware C-Corp formation
   - Order registered agent service ($50/yr)
   - Prepare Certificate of Incorporation
   - File Monday morning
