Energy in moving air = ½mv²

To capture this energy:
- Air enters at 29 m/s (65 mph)
- Turbine extracts energy
- Air exits at lower speed (MUST be slower!)

Energy extracted = ½m(v₁² - v₂²)
Momentum change = m(v₁ - v₂)
Force on turbine = Momentum change / time = DRAG

Key insight: If air doesn't slow down (v₁ = v₂), 
then NO energy extracted (E = 0)
