California Dealer Logistics Solutions (CDLS) is building the world's first decentralized, AI-powered zero-emission automotive logistics network, combining edge computing, distributed ledger technology, and advanced cryptography to transform California's $2+ billion vehicle hauling market.

PROBLEM
California's Advanced Clean Fleets (ACF) mandate requires 100% zero-emission automotive logistics by 2035, yet no existing infrastructure can efficiently manage electric vehicle fleets at scale. Traditional centralized logistics platforms create data sovereignty concerns for dealerships while lacking the computational architecture necessary for real-time, battery-constrained route optimization. The industry needs a solution that preserves dealer data privacy while enabling network-wide optimization.

SOLUTION: THE JUDAS ARCHITECTURE
CDLS has developed a proprietary edge-computing framework where AI inference runs locally on Ollama nodes behind each partner's firewall, eliminating centralized data aggregation risks. Our Python-based JUDAS (Joint Unified Dealer Autonomous System) engine processes raw dealership management system (DMS) data locally to generate optimization hashes, transmitting only non-PII metadata to our distributed ledger.

The architecture delivers:
• 20-25% reduction in empty miles through ML-powered route optimization
• 15-20% improvement in fleet utilization via predictive scheduling algorithms  
• 80-90% reduction in manual coordination through automated load batching
• Zero centralized storage of dealer PII or financial records

Technical Foundation:
• Edge AI inference on local Ollama nodes (NVIDIA Jetson deployment roadmap)
• Distributed ledger ($HAUL and $CARBON tokens) with HSM-secured signing
• TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy for all API communications
• AES-256-GCM encryption at rest
• OAuth 2.0 with Mutual TLS (mTLS) authentication
• SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 alignment architecture

MARKET OPPORTUNITY
