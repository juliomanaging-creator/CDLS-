SUPPLEMENT WITH CHELATING AGENTS:

1. EDTA (Ethylenediaminetetraacetic Acid):
   - Add to copper solution: 0.1 mM EDTA
   - Forms Cu-EDTA complex (stable, low toxicity)
   - Plant absorbs Cu-EDTA (chelated form)
   - Result: Copper conductivity maintained, toxicity reduced 50%! ✅

2. Citric Acid (Natural, Food-Safe):
   - Add lemon juice to copper solution (1 tablespoon per liter)
   - Citric acid binds copper (copper-citrate complex)
   - Plant absorbs, stores safely
   - Bonus: Citric acid is EDIBLE (vitamin C source!)

3. Humic Acid (Soil Amendment):
   - Add to soil: 1-2% humic acid by weight
   - Humic acid binds copper (prevents free ions)
   - Slow-release copper (gradual uptake, less stress)
   - Organic, safe for wildlife

Enhanced Safety Protocol:
- Week 2: Add 10 ppm copper + 0.1 mM EDTA (chelated)
- Week 3: Add 50 ppm copper + citric acid (natural chelator)
- Week 4: Add 100 ppm copper + humic acid (soil buffer)
- Result: Same conductivity (200-500 S/m), 50% less free copper! ✅
