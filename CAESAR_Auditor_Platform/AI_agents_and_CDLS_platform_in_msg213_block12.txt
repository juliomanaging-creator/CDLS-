Application: Grid electricity (stationary)
System:
- Gas turbine: 1,300°C exhaust
- Steam turbine: Uses waste heat from gas turbine
- Efficiency: 60-62% (best in world!)

Why it works:
- Stationary (no weight penalty)
- Large scale (economies of scale)
- Fuel adds energy (not extracting from motion)
- Cooling tower nearby (infinite heat sink)
