Exploit plant's own metabolic processes:

PLANT METABOLISM GENERATES ELECTRONS:
- Photosynthesis: H2O + CO2 → glucose + O2 (produces electrons!)
- Cellular respiration: Glucose → ATP (electron transfer chain)
- Redox proteins: Naturally transport electrons

MICROBIAL FUEL CELL ANALOGY:
- Bacteria in soil oxidize organic matter (release electrons)
- Electrons flow through external circuit (generate current)
- Similar principle can work with plant roots!

PLANT-BASED BIO-BATTERY:
1. Genetically engineer plant (or use natural variants):
   - Overexpress cytochrome proteins (electron carriers)
   - Enhance root exudation (organic compounds released to soil)
   
2. Connect electrodes:
   - Anode: Embedded in root system (collects electrons from metabolism)
   - Cathode: In soil (accepts electrons, reduces oxygen)
   
3. Electron flow:
   - Photosynthesis generates electrons in leaves
   - Electrons transported to roots via plant's vascular system
   - Anode collects electrons (generates current!)
   
4. Continuous power:
   - As long as plant photosynthesizes (sunlight), generates power
   - Self-charging battery! ☀️🔋

Output (research data):
- Power density: 1-10 mW/m² root surface area
- For 100g root (~0.1 m² surface): 0.1-1 mW continuous power
- Daily energy: 2.4-24 Wh (if operates 24 hours)

Advantage: SELF-CHARGING (sunlight → electricity via plant!) ✅
Disadvantage: Low power (suitable for sensors, not high-power applications)
