IRS Classification: Property (like stock)

Taxable Events:
1. Token Sale Purchase:
   ├─ Not taxable (purchase of property)
   └─ Basis: Purchase price

2. Token Appreciation:
   ├─ Not taxable until sold (like stock)
   └─ Capital gains when realized

3. Token Sale:
   ├─ Capital gain/loss
   ├─ Short-term (<1 year): Ordinary income rates
   └─ Long-term (>1 year): 0-20% capital gains rates

4. Staking Rewards ($HAUL):
   ├─ Taxable as income when received
   ├─ Fair market value at receipt
   └─ Basis for future sale

5. Revenue Share (USDC):
   ├─ Taxable as dividend income
   └─ Reported on 1099-DIV

Example:
Buy $CDLS at $0.05 (Jan 2026)
Hold 18 months
Sell at $1.00 (July 2027)
Gain: $0.95 per token
Tax: 15% long-term capital gains = $0.1425 per token
