How it works:
- Broker charges $300-$500 PER CAR
- Price varies by distance (e.g., Sacramento-LA = $400/car)
- Dealer pays per car moved

Example calculation:
- Dealer needs to move 200 cars/year
- Route: Sacramento → LA (350 miles)
- Broker charges: $400/car
- Annual cost: 200 cars × $400 = $80,000
