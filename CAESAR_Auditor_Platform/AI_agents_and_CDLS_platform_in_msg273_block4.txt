🌾 SACRAMENTO FARMERS MARKETS (Weekend Option)

📍 MIDTOWN FARMERS MARKET
When: Saturday 8am-1pm
Where: 20th Street between J & K Streets, Sacramento
Vendors: Local seed savers, heirloom growers
Look for: "Seed Exchange" vendors
Price: $3-6 per packet (often heirloom/organic)
Bonus: Talk directly to growers (best growing advice!)

📍 SUNDAY CERTIFIED FARMERS MARKET
When: Sunday 8am-12pm
Where: 8th & W Streets, Downtown Sacramento
Similar vendors, local seed selection
