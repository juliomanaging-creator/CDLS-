IRS Classification: Property (commodity)

Taxable Events:
1. Generating Credits:
   ├─ Not taxable (similar to mining)
   └─ Basis: $0 (self-created)

2. Selling Credits:
   ├─ Ordinary income (if business)
   ├─ Or capital gains (if investment)
   └─ Likely ordinary income for dealers

Example:
Generate 1,000 LCFS credits
Sell for $105,000
Ordinary income: $105,000
Tax (37% top bracket): $38,850
Net proceeds: $66,150
