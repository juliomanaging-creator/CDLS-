PLANTS PRODUCE COMPOUNDS THAT "TRAP" METALS:

Phytochelatins (Plant-Produced Peptides):
- Structure: (γ-Glu-Cys)n-Gly (amino acid chains)
- Function: Binds Cu²⁺, Zn²⁺, Cd²⁺ (forms stable complex)
- Result: Copper becomes NON-REACTIVE (can't generate ROS!)
- Storage: Chelated copper stored in vacuoles

Organic Acids (Citric Acid, Malic Acid, Oxalic Acid):
- Function: Forms copper-citrate, copper-malate complexes
- Result: Reduces free Cu²⁺ ions (lower toxicity)
- Example: Spinach naturally high in oxalic acid (binds metals safely)

Metallothioneins (Metal-Binding Proteins):
- Structure: Cysteine-rich proteins
- Function: Binds copper tightly (1 protein binds 7-12 Cu atoms!)
- Result: Copper sequestered, not bioavailable

KEY INSIGHT: If copper is CHELATED, it's less toxic even at high concentrations!
