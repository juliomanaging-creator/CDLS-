Annual miles: 10 vehicles × 50,000 miles = 500,000 miles

Diesel emissions avoided:
500,000 miles × 22.4 lbs CO2/mile ÷ 2000 lbs/ton = 5,600 metric tons CO2

LCFS credits generated:
5,600 tons × 0.89 credit intensity factor = 4,984 credits

Revenue at current prices:
4,984 credits × $100/credit = $498,400/year

Revenue at projected 2027 prices:
4,984 credits × $150/credit = $747,600/year
