REQUIRED NUTRIENT:
- Humans need: 0.9 mg/day copper (essential trace mineral)
- Involved in: Hemoglobin synthesis, nerve function, immune system
- Plants need: 5-20 ppm copper in tissues (photosynthesis, cell walls)

The key: DOSE makes the poison!
- 5-20 ppm: Essential nutrient ✅
- 100-500 ppm: Potentially toxic ⚠️
- 1,000+ ppm: Definitely toxic ❌

Goal: Keep copper in "essential" range while maintaining conductivity!
