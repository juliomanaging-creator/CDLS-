Register with SEC under Regulation A+:

Process:
1. Hire securities lawyer ($500K+)
2. Draft offering circular (like prospectus)
3. File Form 1-A with SEC
4. SEC review (3-6 months, multiple rounds)
5. Qualification (approval to sell)
6. Ongoing reporting (annual, semi-annual)

Benefits:
✓ Full legal compliance
✓ Can market to US retail
✓ Institutional confidence
✓ Clear regulatory path

Drawbacks:
✗ Expensive ($2M+ total)
✗ Slow (6-12 months)
✗ Ongoing compliance ($500K/year)
✗ Less "crypto native" appeal

Limits:
- Max raise: $75M in 12 months (Reg A+ Tier 2)
- Quarterly reporting required
- Audited financials
