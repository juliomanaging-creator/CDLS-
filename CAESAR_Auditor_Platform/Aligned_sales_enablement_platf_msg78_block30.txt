Initial: 100M tokens
Use Cases:
├─ Earn: Complete hauls, generate credits, refer dealers
├─ Spend: Pay subscriptions, marketplace fees, governance

Why This Works:

- Creates network effects (viral growth)
- Aligns incentives (everyone benefits from activity)
