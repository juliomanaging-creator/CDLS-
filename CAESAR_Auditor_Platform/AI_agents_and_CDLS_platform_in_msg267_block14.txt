WEEK 1: GERMINATION (same as copper protocol)

WEEK 2-4: CARBON NANOTUBE INTRODUCTION
Prepare CNT solution:
1. Multi-walled carbon nanotubes (MWCNT): 100 mg/L
2. Surfactant (Tween-20): 1 g/L (helps dispersion)
3. Sonication: 30 minutes (ultrasonic bath, disperses nanotubes)
   - Don't have ultrasonic bath? Vigorous shaking for 10 minutes works!

Application:
METHOD A (Hydroponic - Recommended):
- Grow plant in water (no soil)
- Add CNT solution to hydroponic reservoir
- Roots absorb nanotubes over 7-14 days
- Visible result: Roots turn dark gray/black (carbon uptake!)

METHOD B (Foliar Spray):
- Spray CNT solution on leaves (2× per week)
- Stomata absorb nanotubes
- Slower transport to roots (10-14 days)

WEEK 5-6: MATURATION & TESTING
- Roots should be dark-colored (carbon visible!)
- Test conductivity (same method as copper protocol)
- Expected: 1,000-10,000 S/m (higher than copper!) ✅

BONUS: Photosynthesis enhancement!
- Measure plant growth (height, leaf area)
- CNT plants should be 20-30% larger (MIT study confirmed)
- Healthier, faster-growing plants! 🌱

SAFETY:
- CNTs are potentially irritating (wear mask when handling powder)
- Low toxicity (carbon-based, biocompatible)
- Dispose of plant material carefully (CNTs don't biodegrade easily)
