engagement_events
- id (BIGSERIAL, PK)
- dealroom_id (FK)
- user_id (FK - investor user)
- event_type (ENUM: view, download, share, time_spent, scroll_depth)
- entity_type (ENUM: document, section, calculator, page)
- entity_id (UUID)
- metadata (JSONB - page_number, scroll_percentage, etc.)
- duration_seconds (INTEGER - for time_spent events)
- timestamp (TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE)
- session_id (UUID)
- ip_address (INET)
- user_agent (TEXT)

engagement_scores
- dealroom_id (FK, PK)
- calculated_at (TIMESTAMP)
- overall_score (INTEGER 0-100)
- document_engagement_score (INTEGER)
- calculator_engagement_score (INTEGER)
- communication_score (INTEGER)
- trend (ENUM: increasing, stable, decreasing)
- risk_level (ENUM: low, medium, high)
- insights (JSONB - array of insight objects)

heatmaps
- id (UUID, PK)
- document_id (FK)
- page_number (INTEGER)
- heatmap_data (JSONB - x,y coordinates with intensity)
- generated_at (TIMESTAMP)
