"Price Floor Protection":

Treasury Buyback:
├─ If token drops >50% in 7 days
├─ Treasury buys $100K/day
├─ Stabilizes price, builds confidence

Dealer Put Option:
├─ Dealers can sell back to treasury
├─ At 50% of purchase price (guaranteed)
├─ Maximum: 10% of holdings per quarter
├─ Prevents panic selling

Example:
Dealer bought 100K $CDLS at $0.05 ($5,000)
Price crashes to $0.02 ($2,000 loss)
Dealer can sell 10K $CDLS at $0.025 ($250 vs. $200)
Small protection, but builds trust
