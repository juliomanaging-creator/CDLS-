□ File Form LLC-4/7 (Certificate of Dissolution)
   - Online: https://bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov/
   - Fee: $0 (if no assets/liabilities)
   - Reason: "Incorrect entity structure, dissolving before operations"

□ File Final Form 568 (LLC tax return)
   - Due: Within 3.5 months of dissolution
   - Show $0 income/expenses (no activity)
   - Fee: $800 minimum franchise tax (unfortunately owed even if dissolved)

□ Close EIN with IRS
   - Send letter to IRS stating LLC dissolved
   - Request EIN closure
   - Keep letter for records
