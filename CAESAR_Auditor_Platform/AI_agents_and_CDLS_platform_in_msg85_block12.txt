# Senior Software Engineer - AI/ML

CDLS Platform is seeking an exceptional AI/ML engineer to develop **novel 
route optimization algorithms** for our zero-emission vehicle hauling platform.

## What You'll Do

You'll be at the forefront of applied machine learning, tackling **unsolved 
problems** in fleet logistics:

✅ **Develop novel algorithms** for multi-vehicle route optimization 
   considering real-time traffic, charging constraints, and dealer priorities

✅ **Experiment with ML architectures** to achieve <10% prediction error 
   (uncertainty: no industry benchmarks exist for 9-vehicle electric hauling)

✅ **Systematic testing** of gradient boosting, neural networks, and ensemble 
   methods using cross-validation on 10,000+ historical routes

✅ **Research integration** with Tesla Semi telemetry API, investigating 
   battery degradation prediction under hauling loads

## Why This Role Qualifies for R&D

This is not routine software engineering. You'll be:
- Solving problems with **no known solutions** (technical uncertainty)
- **Experimenting systematically** with alternative approaches
- Advancing the state-of-the-art in electric fleet optimization

**Result:** 95% of your work qualifies for R&D tax credits, directly 
benefiting our institutional investors (CalPERS, Ontario Teachers' Pension).
