Chapter 01

What is CASAER?

CASAER stands for CA State Auditing Enforcement Reports. It's a tool that helps you find the information you're looking for faster — it doesn't do your job, it makes your job easier.

💡
Think of CASAER as your research assistant. When a hearing requests information from a department, CASAER shows you exactly where to look, what to ask for, and which statutes apply — so you spend less time searching and more time analyzing.
🔍
Enforcement Pathways

Step-by-step investigation guides tied to specific statutes. Select a pathway and CASAER tells you what documents to request and where to look.

📊
Statistical Analysis

Upload a spreadsheet and CASAER runs Benford's Law testing, anomaly detection, and variance analysis — the math happens automatically.

📋
Audit Checklists

Pre-built checklists for every audit type — financial, compliance, performance, IT, and special investigations. Track your progress in real time.

📄
Report Generation

Generate standardized enforcement reports with findings, evidence, and statute citations. Download as PDF or CSV right from your browser.

⚠️
Important: CASAER provides guidance only. It does not make findings, assign fault, or take enforcement actions. Every output requires your professional review and sign-off before it becomes part of an official audit.
Chapter 02

Getting Started

You can be up and running in under five minutes. Here's how.

1

Open CASAER

Server Edition: Navigate to the CASAER URL provided by your IT team (typically https://casaer.auditor.ca.gov) and log in with your state credentials.

Offline Edition: Double-click the CASAER_Offline_Portal.html file on your desktop or USB drive. It opens in any browser — Chrome, Firefox, or Edge all work.

No installation needed for either version. If using the offline edition, you can bookmark it for quick access.
2

Choose Your Department

Click the Departments tab to see all 132 state departments. You can search by name, department code, or filter by risk level (High, Medium, Low). Click any department card to start an audit session for that agency.

Risk levels are based on budget size, audit frequency, and historical finding rates. They're a starting point, not a judgment.
3

Create an Audit Session

On the New Audit tab, select your department, audit type, and paste in the hearing request language or describe your scope. CASAER immediately generates a tailored checklist and suggests relevant enforcement pathways.

Be specific with the scope description — the more detail you provide, the more targeted CASAER's guidance will be.
4

Follow the Path

CASAER will guide you through the rest: which documents to request, which statutes apply, where to run statistical tests, and when to generate your enforcement report. Check off items as you complete them.

You can always switch between tabs freely. Your session and checklist progress are preserved.

Chapter 04

Using Analytics

CASAER includes three built-in statistical tools that require no technical background to use. Here's what each one does and when to use it.

B
Benford's Law Analysis

What it does: Tests whether the first digits of financial amounts follow the expected natural distribution. Genuinely random or organic data follows a predictable pattern where "1" appears first about 30% of the time, "2" about 17.6%, and so on. If data deviates significantly from this pattern, it may have been fabricated or manipulated.

When to use it: Any time you have a column of dollar amounts — vendor payments, expense reports, contract values, grant disbursements. Works best with 50+ transactions.

How to read results: A green result means the data follows expected patterns. A red result means significant deviation was detected — look at which digits are off and investigate those transaction groups.

!
Anomaly Detection

What it does: Identifies individual transactions that are statistically unusual compared to the rest of the data. Uses two methods: Z-score (how many standard deviations from the average) and IQR (interquartile range outlier detection).

When to use it: After Benford analysis flags a dataset, or whenever you want to find the specific transactions worth investigating. Also useful for overtime analysis, travel expenses, and vendor payments.

How to read results: Each flagged transaction shows its Z-score. Higher Z-scores mean more unusual. Start with the highest scores first. "Standard" sensitivity catches more items; "Strict" shows only extreme outliers.

%
Variance Analysis

What it does: Compares budgeted amounts against actual spending, line by line. Calculates dollar and percentage variances. Flags any line item where actual spending differs from budget by more than 10%.

When to use it: For budget audits, performance audits, and any time a hearing request asks about a department's spending versus their appropriation.

How to read results: Red-highlighted rows are over 10% variance. Positive variance means overspending; negative means underspending. Both extremes can indicate issues worth investigating.

Uploading Data

1

Prepare Your File

Export the relevant data from your working papers into a CSV file. The file should have a column of dollar amounts. CASAER will automatically find and extract the numeric values.

2

Upload or Paste

In the Analytics tab, either click the file upload button to select your CSV, or paste amounts directly into the text box (one per line). Both methods work identically.

3

Run and Review

Click the analysis button. Results appear instantly with charts, tables, and a downloadable CSV of findings. Everything is processed on your machine — no data is uploaded anywhere.

Your data never leaves your browser. Not to the internet, not to a server, not anywhere. This is built into the architecture.

Chapter 06

Generating Reports

CASAER produces four types of downloadable reports. All reports are generated on your machine and formatted for immediate use.

📄 Enforcement Pathway Report

Complete step-by-step investigation record with all statute citations, document requests made, and enforcement findings. This is your primary deliverable.

📊 Statistical Anomaly Report

Benford analysis results, anomaly flags, and statistical methodology documentation. Ready for inclusion as supporting evidence in your audit file.

📋 Department Scorecard

Risk scoring across financial, compliance, and performance dimensions. Useful for prioritizing follow-up audits and committee presentations.

📝 Legislative Brief

Concise executive summary formatted for committee presentation. Key findings, impact estimates, and recommended actions on one page.

How to Generate a Report

Navigate to the Reports tab, select your report type, and click generate. The file downloads instantly to your computer. You can also generate reports directly from within an enforcement pathway by clicking the button at the bottom of the pathway steps.

💾
Download formats: Reports are available as plain text files (TXT) in the offline version and as PDF in the server version. Statistical results can also be downloaded as CSV for further analysis in Excel.

Chapter 08

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

Does CASAER replace any auditor positions? ▼
No. CASAER is designed to enhance auditor capabilities, not replace them. The system provides guidance, runs calculations, and generates templates — but every finding, conclusion, and report requires a human auditor's professional judgment and sign-off. No positions are affected by this system.
Where does my data go when I upload a file? ▼
Nowhere. In the offline edition, all processing happens inside your web browser on your own computer. The data is never transmitted over a network. In the server edition, data stays within the State Auditor's internal network and is processed on the CASAER server — which has no outbound internet connection. Either way, your data never leaves the state's control.
Can I use CASAER results as evidence in an audit? ▼
CASAER outputs are supporting analysis tools, similar to using Excel or statistical software. The statistical results (Benford analysis, anomaly scores) can be included in working papers as analytical evidence, but they should be documented as computer-assisted audit techniques with your methodology notes, just as you would with any other tool.
What does the AI do? Should I trust it? ▼
The AI component (only available in the server edition) processes natural language questions and returns structured guidance based on California statutes and audit standards. It runs on a local model called Ollama — no cloud services, no OpenAI, no data going to any tech company. That said, treat AI outputs the same way you'd treat a research memo from a junior analyst: useful starting point, always verify before relying on it.
How accurate is the Benford's Law analysis? ▼
The mathematical calculations are exact — the same results you would get in R, SAS, or any statistical package. However, interpreting the results requires professional judgment. A "significant deviation" doesn't automatically mean fraud; it means the data pattern is unusual and warrants further investigation. Natural explanations (small datasets, limited transaction types) should be considered.
Can our IT team customize the portal design? ▼
Yes. The system is designed with full customization in mind. Your IT team can change the portal name, branding, colors, layout, and department groupings through the settings panel (no coding required) or build an entirely custom frontend using the documented API. The default CASAER design is production-ready if no customization is desired.
What if I find something that needs criminal referral? ▼
CASAER's vendor fraud pathway includes guidance on when findings rise to the level of potential criminal activity and the steps for referral to the Attorney General's office per existing State Auditor protocols. CASAER does not make this determination — it flags the criteria and you make the call.
How do I get help if something isn't working? ▼
For the server edition, contact your IT help desk — they have full documentation and a quick command reference. For the offline HTML edition, try closing and reopening the file, or try a different browser. If the issue persists, your IT team can access the troubleshooting section of the IT Documentation Package.
