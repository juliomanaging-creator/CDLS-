PLANT:
- Fast-growing plant with substantial root system
- Recommended: Sunflower (Helianthus annuus) - 2-month growth cycle
- Alternative: Radish (Raphanus sativus) - 1-month cycle, large roots

CHEMICALS:
- Copper sulfate (CuSO4): $15/kg (Amazon, garden stores)
- OR Carbon nanotubes (multi-walled, MWCNT): $50/gram (Sigma-Aldrich)
- Surfactant: Tween-20 or dish soap (helps nanoparticle dispersion)

EQUIPMENT:
- Hydroponic setup OR pots with soil
- Distilled water (chlorine-free, doesn't damage roots)
- Multimeter (measures electrical conductivity)
- pH meter (monitors acidity)
- Grow light (if indoors, 12-16 hours/day)

COST: ~$100 total (reusable for multiple experiments)
