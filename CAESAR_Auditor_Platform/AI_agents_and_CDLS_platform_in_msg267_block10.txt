Problem: CDLS depots need energy storage (grid backup, solar buffering)
Solution: Hydroponic "capacitor forest" using conductive polymer plants!

Concept:
- Grow 1,000 plants hydroponically (controlled environment, maximizes uptake)
- Infuse with polyaniline conductive polymer (300 F/g capacitance)
- Each plant: 200g root mass × 300 F/g = 60,000 F capacitance
- Total forest: 1,000 plants × 60,000 F = 60,000,000 F (60 MF!)
- Voltage: 1.2V (safe for plants)
- Energy stored: E = ½ × 60,000,000 × (1.2)² = 43,200,000 J (12 kWh!)

Use case:
- Solar array generates excess power (midday peak)
- Charge plant capacitor forest (store energy)
- Discharge at night (supplement grid power)
- Cycle: Daily (charge-discharge 365 days/year)

Benefits:
✅ Low cost: $10/kWh (vs $150/kWh lithium-ion!)
✅ Long life: 100,000+ cycles (plants regenerate damaged tissues!)
✅ Eco-friendly: Biodegradable, non-toxic (vs heavy metals in batteries)
✅ Air purification: Plants absorb CO2, release O2 (dual benefit!)

Challenges:
❌ Low energy density: 12 kWh for entire forest (vs 12 kWh in 1 battery)
❌ Maintenance: Plants need water, nutrients, light
❌ Scalability: Requires significant space (greenhouses)

Verdict: NICHE APPLICATION (sensors, backup power), not primary energy storage
