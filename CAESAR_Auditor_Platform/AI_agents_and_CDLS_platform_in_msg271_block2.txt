PLANTS THAT NATURALLY ABSORB METALS SAFELY:

Alyssum bertolonii (Nickel Hyperaccumulator):
- Absorbs: 1% nickel by dry weight (10,000 ppm!)
- Stores: In VACUOLES (cell storage compartments)
- Result: Nickel isolated from metabolic processes
- Edibility: Leaves are TOXIC, but roots/seeds safe!

Mechanism:
1. Root absorbs nickel from soil
2. Transports to leaves via xylem
3. Sequesters in leaf vacuoles (isolated compartments)
4. Prevents nickel from damaging cell machinery
5. Old leaves fall off (removes nickel from plant!)

KEY INSIGHT: Store metals in EXPENDABLE tissues (leaves),
              keep EDIBLE tissues (roots, seeds, fruits) clean! ✅
