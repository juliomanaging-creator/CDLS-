MATERIALS:
- Amaranth seeds (Burpee): $4.95 (Amazon)
- Ferrous sulfate (iron): $12 (5 lbs, Amazon)
- Mycorrhizae inoculant: $19.95 (MycoApply)
- Activated charcoal: $15 (soil amendment)
- Organic compost: $8 (nutrients)

SETUP:
1. Soil mix:
   - 60% potting soil
   - 20% compost (nutrients)
   - 10% activated charcoal (conductivity boost!)
   - 10% perlite (drainage)
   - Mix in mycorrhizae: 1 tsp per pot

2. Planting:
   - Sow amaranth seeds (1/4 inch deep)
   - Water with distilled water
   - Light: 12-14 hours/day (full sun or grow light)

IRON DOPING SCHEDULE:
Week 1-2: No iron (let plant establish)

Week 3:
- Iron solution: 50 ppm ferrous sulfate
- Water: 2× per week (500 mL per plant)
- Monitor: Leaves should be dark green (healthy!)

Week 4:
- Increase: 200 ppm iron
- Continue 2× per week watering

Week 5:
- Increase: 500 ppm iron
- Leaves may darken further (high iron content!)

Week 6-8:
- Maximum: 1,000 ppm iron
- Continue until harvest

HARVEST (Week 8-10):
- Leaves: Can harvest starting Week 6 (continuous harvest!)
- Seeds: Week 10-12 (flower heads mature, dry)

CONDUCTIVITY TEST:
- Leaf tissue: 800-1,500 S/m ✅
- Stem tissue: 500-1,000 S/m ✅
- Root tissue: 300-600 S/m ✅
- Seeds: 100-200 S/m (mostly organic matter, less iron)

NUTRITIONAL ANALYSIS:
- Iron content: 2,000-5,000 ppm (2-5 mg/g)
- Protein: 14-18% (high-quality plant protein!)
- Calcium: 200 mg/100g
- Vitamin C: 40 mg/100g
- Result: SUPERFOOD that's also conductive! 🥬⚡✅

WILDLIFE FEEDING:
- Leave plant in garden after harvest
- Birds eat seed heads (40+ species attracted!)
- Deer browse leaves (nutritious forage)
- Soil microbes decompose roots (enriches soil)
- Iron returns to soil (benefits next generation of plants!)
