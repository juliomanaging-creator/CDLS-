Conductive polymers (polyaniline, polypyrrole) undergo reversible redox reactions:

MECHANISM:
- Oxidation: Polymer loses electrons (stores positive charge)
- Reduction: Polymer gains electrons (stores negative charge)
- Reversible: Can cycle thousands of times!

Advantage over EDLC:
- Higher capacitance: 200-500 F/g (2-5× better!)
- Higher energy density: 10-20 Wh/kg (vs 5 Wh/kg EDLC)

Capacity calculation (same 100g root, pseudocapacitive):
- Root mass: 100 grams
- Capacitance: 300 F/g (with polyaniline)
- Total capacitance: 30,000 F (30 kF)
- Voltage: 1.2 V
- Energy stored: E = ½ × 30,000 × (1.2)² = 21,600 Joules (6 Wh)

Result: 3× more energy than EDLC! Still small, but improving! ✅
