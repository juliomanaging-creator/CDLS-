MYCORRHIZAL FUNGI (Plant Root Symbionts):

How they work:
1. Fungal hyphae colonize plant roots (mutualistic relationship)
2. Fungi absorb copper from soil BEFORE plant does
3. Fungi store copper in fungal tissues (not plant!)
4. Result: Plant gets nutrients (nitrogen, phosphorus), fungi gets sugars
5. Copper stays in fungi, not plant tissues! ✅

Types:
- Arbuscular Mycorrhizae (AM): 80% of plants (including sunflowers!)
- Ectomycorrhizae (EM): Trees (oaks, pines)

Protocol:
1. Mix mycorrhizae into soil at planting (1 tsp per pot)
2. Fungi colonize roots (2-4 weeks)
3. Add copper solution (Week 3+)
4. Fungi buffer copper uptake (absorb excess)
5. Plant tissues: 50% less copper than without fungi! ✅

Result:
- Conductivity maintained (fungi-copper also conductive!)
- Plant copper: 50-200 ppm (safe range)
- Fungal copper: 500-2,000 ppm (isolated in fungal network)
- Edible plant parts SAFE for wildlife! 🦌✅
