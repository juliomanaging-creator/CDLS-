1. FTB Account Setup:
   - Automatic upon California registration
   - Account number on Form 100 filing
   - Used for paying $800 annual franchise tax

2. Online Access:
   - Register at: https://www.ftb.ca.gov/
   - Create MyFTB account
   - Link corporate EIN
   - Used for e-filing tax returns

3. First Filing:
   - Form 100 (CA Corporate Tax) due April 15, 2027
   - Form 100-ES (estimated taxes) due quarterly
   - $800 minimum franchise tax (due by 15th day of 4th month)
