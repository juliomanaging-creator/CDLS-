   - Online: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online

□ File Foreign Corporation Registration in California
   - Form S&DC S-100 (Statement of Foreign Corporation)
   - Fee: $100
   - Required within 90 days of doing business in CA
