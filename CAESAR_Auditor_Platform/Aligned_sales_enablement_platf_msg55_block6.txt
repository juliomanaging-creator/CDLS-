San Diego Auto Transport Success Story

Challenge:
"We had 8 aging diesel haulers and were facing CARB compliance pressure. 
EV conversion seemed too expensive and risky."

Solution:
"The CDLS calculator showed us we'd save $255K annually and pay back 
the EV premium in 3 months when including carbon credits."

Results:
- Purchased 4 EV haulers (50% fleet conversion)
- Annual savings: $127K (first 6 months)
- Carbon credits sold: $237K (Q1-Q2 2025)
- Total payback: 2.1 months
- Now expanding to 100% EV fleet
