Process:
1. Buyer accumulates 1,000 credits on-chain
2. Requests redemption (burn NFTs)
3. CDLS submits transfer to CARB
4. CARB processes (3-5 business days)
5. Credits appear in buyer's CARB account
6. NFTs permanently burned

Why Buyers Redeem:
✓ Regulatory compliance (oil companies must)
✓ Corporate ESG reporting
✓ Long-term holding (prefer CARB custody)

Why Buyers Keep On-Chain:
✓ Trading flexibility (24/7)
✓ Collateral for DeFi (borrow against credits)
✓ Speculation (price appreciation)
