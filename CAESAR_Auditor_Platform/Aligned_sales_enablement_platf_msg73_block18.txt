Advantages of Public Blockchain:
✓ All transactions visible
✓ Audit trail immutable
✓ Can trace fund flows
✓ Easier compliance than TradFi

Challenges:
⚠️ Privacy concerns (public addresses)
⚠️ Mixer/tumbler detection
⚠️ Cross-chain tracking

Solution:
- Use address clustering (Chainalysis)
- Flag high-risk addresses
- Require KYC for large withdrawals
