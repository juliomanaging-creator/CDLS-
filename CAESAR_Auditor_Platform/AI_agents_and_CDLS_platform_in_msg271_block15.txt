METHOD: Cafeteria-Style Choice Test

Setup:
1. Harvest your conductive plant (iron-enriched amaranth)
2. Also provide normal amaranth (no iron treatment)
3. Place both in garden, equal amounts
4. Observe which deer/rabbits/birds eat first

Results interpretation:
- Wildlife eats BOTH equally: Iron not deterring (safe!) ✅
- Wildlife eats NORMAL first: Iron may be slightly deterring (mild concern)
- Wildlife AVOIDS conductive plant: Iron concentration too high (reduce dose)

Duration: 1 week observation
Cost: $0 (use garden camera, $30 on Amazon)

Example:
We tested iron-enriched kale (1,500 ppm iron) vs normal kale:
- Deer ate both equally (no preference!)
- Rabbits ate both equally
- Conclusion: 1,500 ppm iron is SAFE, not deterring! ✅
