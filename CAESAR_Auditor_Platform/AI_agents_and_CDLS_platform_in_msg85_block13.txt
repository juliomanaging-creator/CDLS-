"Always ask: What didn't I know in advance? What did I experiment with?

rd_tracker end SESSION_ID \
  --activity "Learned R&D tracking system, tested session logging" \

  --experiment "Practiced logging with supervisor guidance"

"Log daily (5 min at end of day). I review team summaries Fridays. Your 
R&D % should track to 95%. Questions? #engineering-rd-tracking Slack."
