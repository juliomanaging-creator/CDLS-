   GENERAL QUESTIONS:
   
   Q1: What is tokenization?
   A: Tokenization means representing ownership as digital tokens 
   instead of traditional shares. Tokens can be bought, sold, and 
   traded 24/7 on exchanges, giving you liquidity instead of waiting 
   10 years for an exit.
   
   Q2: Why is CDLS tokenizing?
   A: Three reasons: (1) Gives dealers instant liquidity, (2) Lets 
   us raise capital globally, (3) Creates network effects through 
   reward incentives.
   
   Q3: What are the three tokens?
   A: (1) $CDLS = Ownership & governance, (2) $HAUL = Utility & 
   rewards, (3) $CARBON = Tokenized carbon credits.
   
   Q4: How is this different from traditional equity?
   A: 
   Traditional Equity:
   - Locked for 10+ years
   - No liquidity until exit
   - Passive (no daily benefits)
   - Limited to accredited investors
   
   Tokenized:
   - Trade anytime
   - Instant liquidity
   - Earn rewards daily ($HAUL)
   - Open to anyone (with KYC)

   ensure full compliance with SEC, FinCEN, and CARB regulations.
