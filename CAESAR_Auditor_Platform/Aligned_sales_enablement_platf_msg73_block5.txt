CARB System = Legal Record (source of truth)
Blockchain = Trading Record (marketplace)

How It Works:

Step 1: Generation (Real-Time)
├─ Vehicle drives miles
├─ Telematics logs on-chain
├─ Smart contract calculates credits
├─ Mint "Pre-Verified" NFT
└─ Status: Not yet legal LCFS credit

Step 2: Quarterly CARB Reporting
├─ Submit data to CARB (traditional process)
├─ CARB verifies (45-60 days)
├─ Receive official LCFS credits in LRT-CBTS
├─ Smart contract upgrades NFT to "Verified"
└─ Status: Now legal LCFS credit

Step 3: Trading (24/7 on Blockchain)
├─ Dealer lists verified NFT on marketplace
├─ Buyer purchases NFT
├─ Ownership on blockchain changes
└─ Status: Pre-settlement (pending CARB transfer)

Step 4: CARB Settlement (Quarterly)
├─ CDLS batches all blockchain trades
├─ Submits transfer requests to CARB
├─ CARB processes batch
├─ NFT burned (credit now in buyer's CARB account)
└─ Status: Final settlement

Key Principle: Blockchain is "internal ledger"
CARB sees only final settled positions
