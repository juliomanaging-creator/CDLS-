The CDLS Logistics Platform is a Python-based fleet management and optimization system purpose-built for zero-emission automotive hauling operations. Developed by founder-CEO Julio Garcia (technical background, 14+ years automotive industry experience), the platform addresses the operational complexity of managing electric vehicle logistics at scale.

CORE TECHNOLOGY COMPONENTS

1. Route Optimization Engine
Our proprietary algorithms process multi-variable optimization problems unique to electric vehicle hauling: battery state of charge, charging station locations and availability, traffic patterns, delivery time windows, vehicle payload capacity, and weather conditions affecting range. Based on dealer survey data, the system achieves 20-25% reduction in empty miles compared to traditional diesel routing approaches, directly improving profitability while maximizing vehicle utilization.

The route engine runs real-time recalculation as conditions change, dynamically adjusting routes when charging stations are occupied, traffic incidents occur, or priority deliveries are added to the queue.

2. Predictive Fleet Management
Machine learning models analyze vehicle performance data to forecast optimal charging schedules, predict maintenance requirements, and maximize daily vehicle utilization. The system balances competing objectives: minimizing charging time, avoiding peak electricity rates, ensuring vehicles are charged for next-day routes, and extending battery lifespan through optimal charge/discharge cycles.

Survey data indicates our approach delivers 15-20% improvement in fleet utilization versus manual scheduling methods used by traditional haulers.

3. Automated Inventory & Coordination System
The platform eliminates 80-90% of manual coordination tasks between haulers and dealerships through automated vehicle tracking, digital inventory management, and API-based dealer system integration. Computer vision capabilities (planned for NVIDIA Jetson deployment) will automate vehicle condition documentation, VIN verification, and damage detection.

4. Real-Time Analytics Dashboard
Comprehensive operational visibility covering fleet location, battery status, charging state, route progress, delivery ETAs, and performance metrics. The dashboard enables data-driven decision-making and provides dealership customers with real-time shipment tracking.
