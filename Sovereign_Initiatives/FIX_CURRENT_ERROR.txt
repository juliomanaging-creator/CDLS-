STEP 1: Open File Explorer
   Press Windows Key + E

STEP 2: Navigate to your Downloads folder
   Usually: C:\Users\User\Downloads

STEP 3: Find CA-Dealer-Logistics-COMPLETE-PACKAGE.zip
   (The file you just downloaded)

STEP 4: Right-click on it → Extract All

STEP 5: IMPORTANT - Change the destination to:
   C:\Projects
   
   NOT: C:\Projects\ev-cost-platform
   JUST: C:\Projects

STEP 6: Check "Show extracted files when complete"

STEP 7: Click "Extract"

STEP 8: After extraction, you might see a folder like:
   C:\Projects\CA-Dealer-Logistics-COMPLETE-PACKAGE\ev-cost-platform\
   
   OR
   
   C:\Projects\ev-cost-platform\ev-cost-platform\

STEP 9: If you see double folders, move the inner one up:
   
   Option A: Cut and paste
   - Open C:\Projects\CA-Dealer-Logistics-COMPLETE-PACKAGE\
   - Right-click on "ev-cost-platform" folder
   - Click "Cut"
   - Go back to C:\Projects\
   - Right-click → Paste
   
   Option B: Rename
   - Rename CA-Dealer-Logistics-COMPLETE-PACKAGE to ev-cost-platform
