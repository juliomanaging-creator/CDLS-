1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & STRATEGIC VISION
The California Digital Logistics System (CDLS) is an integrated clean-energy, fintech, and fleet logistics platform designed to transform Zero-Emission Vehicles (ZEVs) and auto transport infrastructure into a distributed 15 GWh Virtual Power Plant (VPP) and "Energy Bank."

By scaling to 30,000 bidirectional V2G (Vehicle-to-Grid) units and lightweight mobile energy pods across California, CDLS bridges fleet transport efficiency with real-time CAISO power grid monetization while operating well within federal loan caps and DFPI licensing bounds.
