### IP-MAT-001: TRIPLE-BRAIDED COMPOSITE CHASSIS

**Date of Conception:** December 5, 2025  
**Reduction to Practice:** January 20, 2026 (CAD models, FEA analysis)

**Technical Problem:**
- Traditional aluminum chassis: Heavy (reduces payload), corrosion-prone, no integrated sensing
- Carbon fiber monocoque: Expensive, brittle, catastrophic failure modes
- Need: Lightweight, strong, durable, smart (embedded sensors)

**Inventive Solution:**
Continuous 3D-braided composite preform using three materials:
1. **High-modulus carbon fiber:** Load-bearing (tensile strength)
2. **Aramid (Kevlar):** Impact protection (prevents crack propagation)
3. **Basalt fiber:** Thermal stability (resists 800°C+, fire safety)

Manufacturing: Automated 3D braiding machine creates seamless structure (no joints, no fasteners, no corrosion points).

**Novel Element:**
First automotive chassis using three-material 3D braid. Traditional composites use single material (carbon only) or simple layup (not 3D braided). Triple-braid provides:
- 40% weight reduction vs. aluminum
- 3× impact resistance vs. carbon monocoque
- Fire safety compliance (basalt resists thermal runaway)

**Patent Claim:**
"A continuous 3D-braided structural preform for vehicular applications comprising: high-modulus carbon fiber strands providing tensile load bearing, aramid fiber strands providing impact energy absorption, and basalt fiber strands providing thermal stability, wherein the three materials are interwoven in a continuous braiding process creating a monolithic structure without fasteners or adhesive joints."

**Commercial Value:**

### IP-ADAPT-001: SMA-DRIVEN POLYMORPHIC ACTUATION

**Date of Conception:** December 12, 2025  
**Reduction to Practice:** January 23, 2026

**Technical Problem:**
- Fixed trailer geometry: Can't optimize for both highway (aerodynamics) and urban (maneuverability)
- High-speed: Need compressed profile (low drag)
- Low-speed/parked: Need expanded profile (cooling, V2G access)

**Inventive Solution:**
Shape Memory Alloy (Nitinol) strips integrated into composite frame:
1. **Compressed Mode (65+ mph):** SMA contracts, frame narrows by 12%, drag reduces 15%
2. **Neutral Mode (30-65 mph):** Standard geometry
3. **Expanded Mode (parked):** SMA expands, frame widens by 8%, surface area +15% (better cooling)

**Control Logic:**
- Speed sensor triggers mode changes automatically
- Temperature sensor can override (expand for cooling regardless of speed)
- Manual override available for maintenance

**Novel Element:**
First adaptive-geometry vehicle structure using SMA. Prior art: Fixed structures or hydraulic/pneumatic actuation (heavy, complex). SMA provides: lightweight, no hydraulics, passive safety (returns to neutral if power fails).

**Patent Claim:**
"A polymorphic vehicle frame system comprising: composite structural members, shape memory alloy actuators integrated into frame, speed sensors triggering geometric transitions, temperature sensors providing thermal override, and control system commanding three operational states: compressed for aerodynamic efficiency, neutral for standard operation, and expanded for thermal management."

**Commercial Value:**
- Fuel savings: 12-18% on highway → $8,500/trailer/year × 5,000 = $42.5M/year
- CDLS operational savings: $42.5M/year × 10 years = $425M
