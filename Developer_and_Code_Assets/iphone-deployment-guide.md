# iPhone Deployment Guide for Dealership Workflow

## Quick Start Guide

### 1. Initial Setup (2 minutes)
1. Open Safari on your iPhone
2. Visit the deployment URL
3. Tap the Share button (box with arrow)
4. Select "Add to Home Screen"
5. Name it "DealerFlow"
6. Tap "Add"

### 2. First Launch (1 minute)
1. Locate the DealerFlow icon on your home screen
2. Tap to open
3. Allow notifications when prompted
4. Login with your dealer credentials
