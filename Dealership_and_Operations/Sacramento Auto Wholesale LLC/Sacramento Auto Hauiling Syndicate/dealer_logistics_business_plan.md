## RISK ANALYSIS

### Critical Success Factors

1. **Volume Commitment Validation** (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
   - Must secure signed contracts with volume minimums
   - Refundable deposits demonstrate dealer commitment
   - Without binding agreements, entire model collapses

2. **Capital Adequacy**
   - $100K minimum required for sustainable launch
   - Undercapitalization = 85% probability of failure
   - Must secure gap funding before equipment commitment

3. **Operational Execution**
   - Driver reliability and retention
   - Equipment uptime and maintenance
   - On-time service delivery to maintain dealer satisfaction

4. **Payment Collection**
   - Enforce Net 30 terms strictly
   - One slow-paying large dealer ($20K+/month) can create cash crisis
   - Need 60-day working capital buffer for payment float

### High-Probability Failure Modes

**1. Undercapitalization (85% probability with $41K only)**
- Symptoms: Cannot make Month 2 payroll or lease payments
- Timeline: Failure in 30-60 days
- Mitigation: Secure $60K additional capital before launch

**2. Volume Shortfall (60% probability without binding contracts)**
- Symptoms: Dealers commit 325/month but deliver 150-200
- Impact: Below break-even, monthly losses $5-15K
- Mitigation: Signed contracts with deposits and minimum volume guarantees

**3. Driver Problems (50% probability)**
- Symptoms: Turnover, accidents, damage claims, unreliability
- Impact: Service disruption, reputation damage, recruiting costs $10-15K
- Mitigation: Thorough screening, competitive compensation, backup driver roster

**4. Equipment Failure (40% probability with used equipment)**
- Symptoms: Major breakdown (transmission, engine)
- Impact: $15-30K repair, 50% capacity loss for 2-4 weeks
- Mitigation: Thorough pre-purchase inspection, maintenance reserves, lease vs purchase

**5. Price Competition (40% probability)**
- Symptoms: Incumbents match your 25% discount
- Impact: Margin compression, price war
- Mitigation: Lock dealers into 12-month contracts, differentiate on service quality

**6. Working Capital Crunch (35% probability)**
- Symptoms: Dealers pay Net 45-60, you can't cover weekly payroll/expenses
- Impact: Miss payments, service disruption, potential bankruptcy
- Mitigation: Factor invoices, establish line of credit, enforce payment terms

**7. Regulatory Issues (25% probability)**
- Symptoms: DOT audit, insurance claim denial, license suspension
- Impact: Out-of-service order, fines, loss of dealer confidence
- Mitigation: Strict compliance program, experienced advisor, proper insurance coverage

### Medium-Probability Failure Modes

**8. Insurance Cost Overruns (30% probability)**
- Actual premiums exceed estimates by 30-50%
- Monthly burn increases by $2-3K
- Mitigation: Get binding quotes before committing capital

**9. Fuel Cost Volatility (30% probability)**
- Diesel prices spike 25-50%
- Erodes margins by $10-25 per vehicle
- Mitigation: Fuel surcharge clause in contracts, lock in fuel card discounts

**10. Dealer Financial Distress (20% probability)**
- One or more dealers face cash flow crisis
- Slow payment or default on receivables
- Mitigation: Credit checks, diversification across 5+ dealers, personal guarantees

### Low-Probability, High-Impact Failure Modes

**11. Major Accident/Liability Claim (10% probability)**
- Driver causes serious accident with customer vehicles
- Multi-vehicle damage claim exceeding insurance
- Potential lawsuit and reputation destruction
- Mitigation: Thorough driver screening, safety protocols, adequate insurance limits

**12. Economic Recession (15% probability in 2025)**
- Dealer wholesale volumes decline 30-50%
- Your volume drops below break-even
- Mitigation: Cost structure flexibility, focus on high-volume dealers, geographic expansion
