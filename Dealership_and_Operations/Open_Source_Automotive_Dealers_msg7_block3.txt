Improved Close Rate (ICR) = 
    Current Close Rate * (1 + Expected Improvement %)

Additional Monthly Sales (AMS) = 
    Current Lead Volume * (ICR - Current Close Rate)

CRM Revenue Impact = 
    AMS * (Average Front Gross + Average Back Gross)

CRM ROI = 
    ((CRM Revenue Impact * 12) - Annual CRM Cost) / Annual CRM Cost * 100
