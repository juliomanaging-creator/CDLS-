UNIVERSAL INSURANCE COVERAGE. STATE ENTERPRISE. INITIATIVE STATUTE.

Establishes Unified Shield Authority, a state insurance enterprise 
providing mandatory health, auto, and property coverage for all California 
residents. Requires minimum coverage; imposes tax penalty for non-compliance. 
Premium costs based on income. Authorizes $30 billion in general obligation 
bonds for initial funding. Establishes catastrophic coverage pool. Allows 
supplemental private insurance.

Summary of estimate by Legislative Analyst and Director of Finance of 
fiscal impact on state and local government: Increased state costs of 
approximately $25 billion annually for program administration and coverage, 
funded by premiums and bond proceeds. Increased state revenue of approximately 
$15 billion annually from penalties on non-compliance. Net fiscal impact 
depends on federal waiver approvals and premium collection rates.
