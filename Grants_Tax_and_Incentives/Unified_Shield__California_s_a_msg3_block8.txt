SECTION 1: PROJECT OVERVIEW
Independent actuarial review of Unified Shield comprehensive insurance 
product for 100% California population coverage.

SECTION 2: SCOPE OF WORK
Task 1: Premium Adequacy Analysis
- Validate loss ratio assumptions (health 82%, auto 70%, home 65%)
- Review risk adjustment methodologies
- Assess geographic rating factors
- Stress test pricing model (±15% claims variance)

Task 2: Reserve Adequacy Analysis  
- Initial capitalization ($79B) sufficiency review
- Catastrophic scenario modeling (1-in-50, 1-in-100, 1-in-200 year)
- Concurrent event analysis (earthquake + wildfire + pandemic)
- Reserve development projections (5-year)

Task 3: Solvency & Sustainability
- Cash flow modeling (Years 1-10)
- Surplus adequacy by year
- Required reinsurance coverage analysis
- Sensitivity analysis (enrollment variance, medical inflation)

Task 4: Comparative Market Analysis
- Current CA insurance market benchmarking
- Administrative cost validation (12% claim vs. 15-20% market)
- Consumer savings verification

Task 5: Regulatory Compliance Review
- CDI solvency requirements (Risk-Based Capital equivalent)
- DMHC tangible net equity standards
- Federal MLR requirements (ACA)

DELIVERABLES:
- Draft report (Week 16)
- Final report with responses to comments (Week 20)
- Executive summary (10 pages, public-facing)
- Technical appendices (detailed models)
- Legislative testimony preparation (2 days)
- Response to regulatory agency questions (ongoing)
