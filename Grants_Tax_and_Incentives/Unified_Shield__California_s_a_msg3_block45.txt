MESSAGE 1: "Stop Overpaying for Insurance"
California families pay $2,400 more per year than necessary because 
insurance companies split our coverage into expensive, confusing pieces. 
Proposition X combines health, auto, and home into one affordable policy.

MESSAGE 2: "Pay What You Can Afford"
Under Proposition X, your premium is based on your income, not insurance 
company profits. Working families pay less. Everyone is covered.

MESSAGE 3: "California Leads Again"
Just like we led on climate action and healthcare, Proposition X makes 
California the first state with true universal coverage. Other states 
will follow our lead.

MESSAGE 4: "Protect Against Disasters"
Wildfires and earthquakes threaten our homes. Insurance companies are 
abandoning California. Proposition X creates a catastrophic protection 
pool so you're never left uncovered.
