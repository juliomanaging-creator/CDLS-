THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA DO ENACT AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1. TITLE
This act shall be known and may be cited as the "California Universal 
Coverage Act of 2026."

SECTION 2. FINDINGS AND DECLARATIONS
The People of California find and declare:

(a) Insurance fragmentation costs California families an average of 
$2,400 annually compared to integrated coverage.

(b) Eleven percent of Californians lack health insurance, fifteen 
percent of motorists are uninsured, and twelve percent of homeowners 
lack adequate property coverage, creating societal costs and personal 
financial devastation.

(c) Climate change and increasing disaster risk make traditional 
insurance markets unstable, requiring collective catastrophic risk 
pooling.

(d) Income-based premium structures ensure universal affordability 
while maintaining actuarial soundness.

(e) Integration of health, auto, and home coverage produces 
administrative efficiencies, coordinated risk management, and superior 
consumer outcomes.

(f) California has constitutional authority and fiscal capacity to 
establish a state insurance enterprise serving all residents.

SECTION 3. PURPOSE AND INTENT
The purposes of this act are to:

(a) Establish universal insurance coverage for all California residents;
(b) Reduce household insurance costs through administrative efficiency;
(c) Create income-based premium structures ensuring affordability;
(d) Build catastrophic resilience through collective risk pooling;
(e) Simplify insurance enrollment and claims processing;
(f) Improve health, safety, and financial security for all Californians.

SECTION 5. REVENUE AND TAXATION CODE AMENDMENTS

Section 17053.XX. Non-Compliance Penalty
(a) Any resident who fails to maintain minimum coverage shall pay 
a penalty of 10% of Modified Adjusted Gross Income.
(b) Penalty collected by Franchise Tax Board.
(c) Revenue deposited in Unified Shield fund.

SECTION 8. SEVERABILITY
If any provision held invalid, remaining provisions remain in effect.

SECTION 9. CONFLICTING MEASURES
If this initiative receives more affirmative votes than any conflicting 
measure, this initiative prevails in its entirety.

SECTION 10. AMENDMENT
This act may be amended by statute passed by 2/3 vote of each house, 
provided amendments further the purposes stated in Section 3.

SECTION 11. LIBERAL CONSTRUCTION
This act shall be liberally construed to effectuate its purposes.

SECTION 12. EFFECTIVE DATE
This act takes effect immediately upon approval by voters.
